## Layout check

View from the visitor entrance at the bottom, looking along the central axis:

| Depth from entrance | Left | Right | World Z |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nearest | Dome 1 | Dome 6 | 18 |
| Middle | Dome 2 | Dome 5 | -2 |
| Farthest | Dome 3 | Dome 4 | -22 |

- The three left centers are at X = -18; the three right centers at X = 18. Opposing pairs share Z. Same-side spacing is consistently 20 scene units.
- The final mandir center is X = 0, Z = -49, with its main entrance facing the arrival end (+Z). The landmark was moved five scene units rearward after checking its stair projection: the stair toe at Z = -36.475 clears the north crosswalk’s outer edge at Z = -36. A level paved connector joins the steps to the walk. It is an architectural landmark, not an additional exhibition section.
- The long rectangular garden occupies X = -9 to 9, Z = -33 to 33. The unchanged central basin occupies X = -2.1 to 2.1, Z = -29 to 23.
- Five evenly spaced fountain groups remain in the basin. Two matching large carved-stone fountains occupy the entrance parterres at X = ±6, Z = 29, with radius 2.1. Their inner edges leave the central mandir sightline open. Balanced lawns, low borders and planting beds flank the axial water garden.
- Broad side walks centered at X = ±11.5 run from Z = -35 to 37 and meet connecting walks at Z = -34 and 35. The near connector moved forward with the garden extension, preserving a continuous route around all water. Every pavilion retains its short inward approach to the nearest side walk.
- Trees sit outside the pavilion fronts and between/outside the buildings. The inward doorways, short approaches and numbering remain clear.
- The SVG and fallback pins expose all six destinations with the same numbering. The architectural geometry and original section identities are unchanged by the diagram.

The schematic is deliberately diagrammatic: pavilion and garden world X is shown at 9 SVG pixels per unit and Z at 6 pixels per unit, to remain legible at the existing 900 × 650 fallback aspect ratio. The compact mandir massing is offset to show the stair-to-crosswalk clearance while preserving its label space; its footprint is not drawn to that coordinate scale. The diagram does not provide construction measurements or verified mandir proportions. The interactive opening camera and final selectable route require separate browser verification.
