## Source boundary

The entire content source remains `docs/Takeaways for gutko.pdf`, page 1. It supplies six takeaway messages; it does not supply full exhibition panels, photographs, approved religious media, a floor plan, festival names, ritual procedures, or detailed narratives. The eighteen “story moments” are short editorial explorations of phrases on that page, not newly invented religious stories.

## First complete world: Hindu Festivals

**World name:** A courtyard of light.

**Concept:** Enter a shaded sandstone doorway and arrive in an intimate open court. A reflecting pool and layered colonnades translate the source’s three victories through warm light, a clear central shaft, and connected stone islands. The camera stays composed while the atmosphere changes.

**Source binding:** The PDF’s section 4 title is “Hindu Festivals”, with the subtitle “Celebrations of Light & Life”. Its takeaway names exactly three victories: light over darkness, truth over ego, and love over separation. The prototype must preserve those phrases. It must not assign them to named festivals or add festival histories, deities, dates, offerings, or rituals.

**Material family:** Warm sandstone `#c69a76`, ivory `#f1e5c9`, restrained aged-gold `#ac803f`. Recessed carving and layered stone edges provide depth. The court is set at dusk, with a quiet pool reflecting the surrounding warm lights. Gold is an accent, not the dominant surface.

**Movement target:** A 1.1-second exterior approach, a brief entrance cue, and a 0.65-second interior settling movement connect map → doorway → courtyard. The reading content appears at arrival. Selecting another moment safely interrupts a previous move. Reduced motion uses an immediate or short transition with the same content and labels. These are configured durations; loading and frame delivery depend on the device. An immediate summary button makes reading independent of arrival.

**Story layering:** The title and 15–25-word caption offer a quick first layer. Activating a story exposes its short summary, one takeaway, and page-1 source reference. The full section summary remains accessible. Story controls are HTML, usable with keyboard and touch, and must not rely on hovering the 3D scene.
