# Hindu Mulyo: photograph provenance

Reviewed 9 November 2026 (America/Toronto).

Four distinct official BAPS event photographs accompany Daya, Kshama, Aadar and Tyag. The card descriptions are the exhibition owner's supplied original exhibition copy. They are not quotations by Mahant Swami Maharaj. The factual photo captions, dates, locations and credits are separate from those descriptions.

## Selection and factual context

The link from a photographed blessing to compassion and from devotional preparation to selflessness is an exhibition interpretation. The captions do not claim an undocumented charitable intervention, disaster-relief activity, meal distribution, quotation, or historical incident. Forgiveness uses the actual documented event, rather than a generic portrait relabelled as that event.

## Assets and inspection

`content/hindu-values-photographs.json` retains each exact original image URL, source page, original caption where present, editorial caption, location, ISO date, display date, credit, permission basis, pixel dimensions, original SHA-256 and contextual qualification.

- `public/values/daya.webp` — 1200 × 800.
- `public/values/kshama.webp` — 1199 × 800.
- `public/values/aadar.webp` — 1200 × 800.
- `public/values/tyag.webp` — 1199 × 800.
- Original downloaded JPGs are retained under `public/values/full/` with the same four names.

These are the full web-resolution files exposed by the selected official galleries; no higher-resolution originals are claimed. They support approximately 600 CSS pixel image areas at 2× density. No upscaling, color grading, retouching, reconstruction or content edits were applied. The WebPs preserve the full frame and original pixel dimensions. All four originals were visually inspected at full tool display size; faces, hands, the child's shoulder blessing, folded-hands greeting, rolling pin and preparation board remain visible. The UI should retain the original aspect ratio without cropping.

## Website-use permission and credit

The existing project record in `docs/design/dome-4-gallery-verification.md` states that the exhibition owner explicitly confirmed permission covers **official BAPS event photos**. The same basis is retained for these four official event photographs. This is recorded owner authorization, not a claim of a public blanket licence or BAPS endorsement. Copyright and the displayed credit remain with **BAPS Swaminarayan Sanstha**.

No photographer name is asserted because the selected pages do not supply one. Official provenance is linked independently of the permission record.
