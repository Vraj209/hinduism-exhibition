# Dome 4 photo research — Vaishakh through Aso

Research completed 9 November 2026, America/Toronto. This note records photo sourcing only; it does not claim complete coverage of the requested 24 entries. The companion `dome-4-photo-candidates-second.json` contains 26 individually selected photographs representing nine of the twelve entries in this half of the year, including distinct Navratri and Dussehra photographs. Image dimensions were read from downloaded source files and all selected images were visually inspected.

## Composition and provenance checks

- Each covered entry's first candidate is the suggested lead. Supplementary images provide a different scale or perspective, and are intended for the expanded gallery.
- Photographs retain the source's vivid natural colour. No generation, compositing, face alteration, ritual substitution or location substitution was performed.
- Source dimensions include 898×719 and 899×781 images, with recent Cambridge and Abu Dhabi photographs reaching 2000px on the long edge; use containment in the expanded view and avoid additional cropping of faces, murtis or ritual objects.
- The 2026 Abu Dhabi gallery contains both Ganesh Utsav and Jal Jhilani photographs. Only the identified Ganesh ceremony and procession photographs were selected for Ganesh Chaturthi.
- Akshaya Tritiya's unusual July date is supported by the official individual caption describing Adhik Maas. Preserve that context in implementation notes and metadata.
- The candidate JSON includes temporary `localPath` values as acquisition handoff information. Do not rely on those temporary paths in the deployed site; copy/process the assets into the application's public asset directory and retain official URLs in permanent provenance metadata.
- The three missing entries above remain internal entries in the requested sequence, with explicit missing-photo status. No visual placeholders should pretend to represent those festivals.

## Search limitations

Searches include official event/news pages and official photograph galleries, not social-media accounts or third-party reposts. A missing result means no qualifying photograph was located in this research pass; it is not a claim that BAPS has never held or photographed that celebration. Direct HTML and image checks used a normal browser user agent where bare HTTP requests returned 403 responses. The public BAPS website contains recent migrated routes and older `.aspx` galleries; both formats were consulted.
