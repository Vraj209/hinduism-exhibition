# BAPS / Akshardham visual revision audit

Research checked 8 November 2026, America/Toronto. This is an audit of the Phase 2 baseline and an implementation brief, not a claim that the proposed revision is already rendered. The companion [asset register](asset-register.md) records intended use and rights status. No official photograph was downloaded into the application or approved for publication during this research.

## Source and scope

The supplied `docs/Takeaways for gutko.pdf` was re-extracted and checked against `docs/exhibition-source-outline.md`. It remains a one-page, six-section source:

| 6     | Hindu Sanskruti Ādhār Stambh | The Three Pillars                       | Shastra, Mandir and Sant — Guru, together in one section.    |

The PDF names **no individual festival**, physical plan, saint, scripture title or approved image. Its Sant / Satpurush wording is a particular devotional account, not an account of every Hindu tradition.

The user's later clarification, **“all hindu festival,”** authorizes a researched expansion of section 4. `content/festivals.ts` therefore adds 14 selected named celebrations, clearly attributed to official BAPS sources. They are not described as contents of the PDF. The original central message remains. The collection is broad, not exhaustive, and it is not a dated festival calendar. Annakut and Gujarati New Year are related but separate editorial entries; Ram Navami and Swaminarayan Jayanti are distinct commemorations. The six top-level destinations remain six.

## Architectural rules for the revision

1. Give columns a readable base, tapered or faceted shaft, carved band and bracketed capital. Avoid relying on a single smooth cylinder and bulbous cap.
2. Make ceilings visibly constructed from concentric courses and radial subdivisions. The direct official images show densely worked, layered surfaces, not smooth hemispheres.
3. Use original floral rosettes, leaves, bead bands and geometric screens with depth. Do not trace a photograph or clone a figural relief. The specific figures in the real ceilings have devotional meanings.
4. Differentiate timber haveli, pale stone mandapam, evening festival court and garden. Colour alone should not distinguish the six worlds.
5. Put story objects at a readable size and position in desktop and mobile compositions. Carving should support the message rather than obscure it.
6. Keep material colour stable under light: warm ivory stone, pink sandstone, carved dark timber and limited brass. Use soft grazing light to reveal relief.
7. Keep text and controls in HTML. The full collection, source references and interpretation notes must remain available without WebGL.

## Festival content and object audit

The first three named entries are Diwali, Annakut and Holi. The remaining eleven broaden the collection without pretending to encompass all traditions. `content/festivals.ts` contains 14 unique IDs and 53–61-word summaries. No current-year dates, fasting prescriptions or festival attendance claims are presented.

These document examples, not mandatory arrangements for every community.

Some older BAPS topic pages describe Pramukh Swami Maharaj in the present tense. Those passages are not used as current-leadership or current-event evidence. The collection avoids conflicting date details and does not repeat source-page errors or broad regional equivalences.

## Rights, credit and implementation boundary

The current [Akshardham terms](https://akshardham.com/terms-and-conditions/) and [BAPS terms](https://www.baps.org/Terms-and-Conditions.aspx) reserve rights in photographs, text, graphics, designs and marks, and restrict copying, modification and publication without the stated permission. [London Mandir image terms](https://londonmandir.baps.org/utilities/terms-of-use/) also limit image use and do not provide a general website-publication licence. A download link, public gallery or credit line is not evidence of permission.

**Proceed with:** original geometry, authored material patterns, independent floral/geometric ornament and new editorial illustrations of documented objects. Use broad architectural vocabulary from the research and an original composition. This does not establish clearance for a close reconstruction of a distinctive building, sculpture or photograph.

**Keep as research links:** all official photographs, photo crops, gallery images, images of murtis/gurus, recordings, logos and artwork. Do not hotlink them into the app, convert them to textures, trace them, or use them as image-editing inputs on the assumption that attribution grants a licence.

If actual photographs are later supplied with permission, record the exact file, owner, written licence, allowed crops/adaptations, credit and display location before replacing an illustration. No permission request or other external message was sent in this task.

Suggested public credit for the original scenes: “Original digital architecture inspired by studies of BAPS mandirs and Swaminarayan Akshardham, New Delhi.” Do not imply BAPS endorsement or that these are photographs of the supplied exhibition.
