# Domed exhibition pavilions — reference and carving plan

Research checked 9 November 2026. This plan separates the verified New Delhi exhibition-hall façade references from the original dome family requested for this digital exhibition. It does not claim that the six pavilions reconstruct six existing Akshardham buildings.

Read this alongside the [thematic relief implementation plan](thematic-relief-plan.md), which specifies how the six carving narratives become raised geometry. This document provides the building-reference evidence and source boundaries.

Its captions distinguish the exhibition hall from the main mandir and surrounding parikrama. ### References explicitly excluded as dome evidence

- The [general Exhibitions banner](https://akshardham.com/newdelhi/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/akshardham_exhibition_banner1.jpg) was visually inspected: it depicts an **indoor staged Vedic bazaar**, not an exhibition-hall exterior.
- The [16 April 2004 observation-tour attachment](https://akshardham.com/47-20f/) and its [photograph](https://akshardham.com/newdelhi/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/47-20f.jpg) show exhibit scenery **inside** a coffered structural hall. Its caption alone must not be mistaken for exterior evidence.
- Image 59 of the BAPS November 2003 gallery, [Hall 2](https://www.baps.org/Data/Sites/1/Media/GalleryImages/2982/WebImages/125-8f.jpg), is an unfinished interior. It was inspected and excluded from roof-profile inference.
- Page 8 of the [official New Delhi guide](https://akshardham.com/newdelhi/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/akshardhambrochureenglish.pdf#page=8) was rendered and inspected. Its bottom panorama shows the water-show court and backdrop, not a verified large exhibition dome. Its historical operating information is not used here.
- The main mandir's shikharas and its interior mandapam ceilings are different subjects. Earlier general Akshardham references do not substitute for exhibition-building exterior evidence in this revision.

**Evidence boundary:** a prominent central dome on the photographed exhibition hall was not verified. The common large dome is therefore an original, user-requested design decision. The verified exhibition façade informs the bases, wall rhythm, projecting bands and carving depth. No dimensions below are measurements of the real hall.

## One coherent family of six domed pavilions

Use one enclosed pavilion shell, one dominant dome profile, one entrance module and one material family for all six destinations. The subjects of the stone reliefs provide the differences. Preserve the six-section campus organization and its existing navigation destinations.

The final digital implementation uses a **7 × 5.8** wall footprint, approximately **8.5 × 7.3** stepped plinth and a **5.8-diameter dome with 2.65 rise** above its banded drum. These are scene units and original design proportions, refined from the initial working dimensions. The rise-to-diameter ratio is about 0.46, giving the dome a broadly hemispherical presence; it is not a measured Akshardham roof profile. All six pavilions retain the same profile. Final placement, entrance views and renderer measurements are recorded in the [campus rendering evidence](domed-campus-rendering.md).

The following relief compositions are editorial interpretations of that text; they are not historical artifacts or claims that these carvings exist at Akshardham.

All six primary motifs should remain recognizable in stone silhouette and shadow before a visitor reads the title. Smaller ornamental panels should repeat the related motif rather than introduce unrelated religious symbols. Written section names, source messages and accessible labels remain separate HTML content.

## Source and publication boundaries

The original six messages and displayed PDF quotations remain unchanged. The PDF's final suggested closing statement remains a suggestion, not an attributed scriptural quotation. The later fourteen-festival extension remains labeled and separately sourced in `content/festivals.ts` and the public references route.

Official photographs are research-only references. No photograph, crop, photo texture, traced carving, official logo or sacred likeness is imported through this plan. See the [BAPS terms](https://www.baps.org/Terms-and-Conditions.aspx) and [Akshardham terms](https://akshardham.com/terms-and-conditions/) for the publishers' notices; a public image URL or acknowledgement is not permission to redistribute it. No permission request or external message was sent.

Suggested public attribution: **“Original domed exhibition pavilions. Façade studies informed by BAPS photographs of Akshardham New Delhi's exhibition hall; dome proportions and thematic reliefs are original interpretations.”** The caption should identify the archival Hall 1 images and avoid suggesting an exact reconstruction or official endorsement.

## Review criteria for the resulting model

1. All six campus buildings read as members of one prominently domed family; the first read is not six unrelated roof types or main-mandir shikharas.
2. Each has the shared plinth, wall rhythm and centered entry, plus a different source-grounded raised relief that is visible during the approach.
3. Entry framing gives the visitor a stable view of the carving before moving through the doorway; transitions must not strand focus or break map return.
4. Exterior labels and public references distinguish verified façade study from original dome proportions.
5. The refreshed campus fallback agrees with the live geometry. Existing 29 interior stories, sources, native reading disclosures and all navigation remain available.

Final implementation and browser evidence will be recorded in `docs/design/domed-pavilions-verification.md` after the geometry and fallback freeze. This document records reference findings and design direction, not a claim that the model or regression checks are already complete.
